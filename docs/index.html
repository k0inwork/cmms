Research & Background

Deep Research
Market analysis comparing CMMS vs FSM vs EAM archetypes. Benchmarks against OxMaint, IFS Cloud, InnoMaint, Dynamics 365, and Aerones. ROI data: 19x cost saving on planned gearbox repairs. Implementation blueprint.
Research
Market context · competitive benchmarks · ROI justification
Open Source CMMS Review
Evaluation of 6 platforms: Atlas CMMS, SuperCMMS, Trier OS, Liberu Maintenance, Snipe-IT, CalemEAM. Gap analysis showing why none fit the wind-energy requirements. Build-vs-borrow decision.
Research
Conclusion: custom build — no OSS platform covers offline + absence pipeline + evidence
Borrowed Patterns
Specific patterns extracted from evaluated platforms: IndexedDB operation queue (Trier OS), idempotent operations, auto-recovery, asset hierarchies (Snipe-IT), form builders (Liberu), and file storage (Atlas).
